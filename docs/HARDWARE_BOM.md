# Hardware Bill of Materials (BOM)

This document specifies all mechanical hardware, fasteners, inserts, casting materials, and filaments required to assemble the **ENPIRE Gripper** and its various robot arm adapter variants.

---

## 1. Fasteners & Mechanical Hardware

| Component | Specification | Quantity (per arm) | Target Application | Recommended Torque |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **M3 Socket Head Cap Screws** | M3 × 12mm (DIN 912 / ISO 4762, Grade 12.9) | 4 | Base mounting for I2RT YAM / Piper fingers | 1.2 Nm |
| **M3 Socket Head Cap Screws** | M3 × 16mm (DIN 912) | 2 | Clamp retention for extended fingers | 1.2 Nm |
| **M3 Button Head Screws** | M3 × 8mm (ISO 7380) | 4 | Low-profile slider retention | 0.8 Nm |
| **M4 Socket Head Cap Screws** | M4 × 16mm (DIN 912, Grade 12.9) | 4 | Robotiq 2F-85 / 2F-140 bracket adapter | 2.5 Nm |
| **M6 Socket Head Cap Screws** | M6 × 16mm (DIN 912) | 4 | ISO 9409-1-50-4-M6 Tool Flange (UR, xArm) | 8.0 Nm |
| **M5 Socket Head Cap Screws** | M5 × 12mm (DIN 912) | 4 | ISO 9409-1-31.5-4-M5 Compact Tool Flange | 4.5 Nm |
| **M3 Threaded Heat-Set Inserts** | M3 × 4.0mm (OD 4.6mm, Brass tapered) | 4–8 | Finger mounting blocks & slider brackets | Heat press at 220°C–240°C |
| **M4 Threaded Heat-Set Inserts** | M4 × 5.0mm (OD 6.0mm, Brass tapered) | 4 | Robotiq adapter bracket base | Heat press at 230°C–250°C |

## 2. 3D Printing Materials

| Material Type | Recommended Brand / Grade | Color | Target Parts | Key Mechanical Properties |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **PETG-CF / PLA-CF** | Bambu Lab PETG-CF / Polymaker PolyMide | Matte Black | Structural finger beams, adapter brackets | High stiffness, low creep, sharp details |
| **Nylon (PA12-CF)** | Bambu PA-CF / Polymaker PA6-CF | Dark Gray | Heavy-duty industrial finger bodies | Superior impact strength & temperature resistance |
| **TPU (Flexible)** | TPU 95A / TPU 85A (NinjaTek / Overture) | Green / Orange | Snap-in high-friction grip pads | Shore 85A–95A, high coefficient of friction ($\mu > 0.8$) |
| **Tough Resin (SLA)** | Formlabs Tough 2000 / Siraya Tech Blu | Translucent / Gray | Precision pinch fingers & micro-clamps | High dimensional accuracy ($\pm 0.05\text{ mm}$) |

## 3. Silicone Casting Supplies (Optional for Soft High-Grip Tips)

For handling glossy, wet, or delicate objects with maximum friction:
- **Silicone Compound**: Smooth-On *Dragon Skin™ 20* or *Ecoflex™ 00-30* (1:1 mix ratio by weight)
- **Mold Release Agent**: Smooth-On *Universal Mold Release* or Mann *Ease Release 200*
- **Mixing / Degassing Equipment**: Disposable mixing cups, stir sticks, and optional vacuum degassing chamber (recommended to remove micro-bubbles)
- **Silicone Pigment**: Silc Pig™ (Electric Green or Signal Red)

---

## 4. Tools Required for Assembly

- 2.0mm & 2.5mm Metric Hex (Allen) Keys / Precision bit driver
- 3.0mm & 5.0mm Hex Keys (for M4 and M6 bolts)
- Soldering iron with M3/M4 conical heat-set insert installation tip
- Digital calipers (0.01mm resolution)
- Loctite 243 (Medium-strength blue threadlocker)
