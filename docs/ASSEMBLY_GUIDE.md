# Assembly & Installation Guide

This guide covers physical hardware assembly, fastening, and gripper mounting for the **Open-ENPIRE-Gripper** family.

---

## 1. Required Hardware & Tools

* **Fasteners**: Metric socket head cap screws (Grade 12.9) — standard M3/M4 depending on arm bracket.
* **Heat-Set Inserts**: M3 / M4 brass threaded inserts.
* **Chemicals**: Medium-strength blue threadlocker (Loctite 243).
* **Tools**:
  * Metric hex key set (2.0 mm, 2.5 mm, 3.0 mm)
  * Soldering iron with flat/conical tip (for heat-set insert press-in)
  * Calipers (0.01 mm precision)
* **3D-Printed Parts**: 3D-printed finger components from [`grippers-stl/`](../grippers-stl/).

---

## 2. Installation Steps

### Step 1: Heat-Set Insert Installation
1. Heat soldering iron to **230°C** for PETG/PLA or **275°C** for PA-CF.
2. Place the M3/M4 insert straight onto the mounting hole.
3. Gently press downward with the iron tip until the insert sits perfectly flush with the plastic face.
4. Allow 60 seconds to cool and lock into place.

### Step 2: Finger Mounting to Gripper Chassis
1. Align the finger adapter mounting counterbores with the gripper slider brackets.
2. Apply a drop of medium threadlocker to the screw threads.
3. Torque fasteners evenly until firm (do not overtighten into plastic).

### Step 3: Stroke & Clearance Check
1. Manually command the gripper from fully open (0 mm) to fully closed (50 mm).
2. Verify that both fingertips meet symmetrically along the center line without binding or rubbing against the actuator body.
